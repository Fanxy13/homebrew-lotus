# Editing

- **Pace**: calm. A scene lasts as long as the voice-over needs; nothing under 3 seconds unless it is a title.
- **Cuts**: hard cut inside a scene only to remove waiting; a 12-frame cross dissolve between scenes; nothing flashy. The lotus mark may
  "bloom" (the mark opening, 1 second) as the transition into scenes 3 and 13 – the same motion as the site.
- **Text**: Instrument Serif italic for titles, JetBrains Mono for commands, Inter for small captions; off-white `#eef2ee` on the dark ground,
  accent `#a6c47a`. Captions appear with the word they belong to, 2 seconds, fade in and out. No emoji.
- **Commands**: when a command is typed, zoom in 110 % on the line (slow ease), back out on the result.
- **Split screen** (scene 6): Mac left, Linux right, a thin line between them, the system name small above each.
- **Music**: ambient, warm, slow (60 to 80 bpm), no drums, no vocals; a soft piano or a pad with a little shimmer, like rain on a pond.
  Duck it 12 dB under the voice-over. License: music you own or a track whose license allows YouTube; keep the license text for the description.
- **Sound**: the typing is real keyboard audio, softened; no whooshes.
- **Subtitles**: English, from `script.md`, as a separate subtitle file (SRT) – not burned in.
- **Export**: 1080p or 4K, H.264 or H.265, 20 Mbit/s or more at 1080p, AAC 320 kbit/s, loudness about -14 LUFS.
